Sept 5, 2025

A small circle of teachers who loved a good puzzle as much as their students did. LogIQids Educators Club was born, on Teachers’ Day, of all days.

Week one

The first Puzzle of the Week landed on a Saturday morning. Nobody was quite sure if a room full of educators would stop to solve riddles on a weekend — you did, over your Saturday chai.

All year long

A new puzzle, every Saturday, without fail. And every time, you showed up — solving, comparing answers, sneaking a few onto your own classroom board.

A note to you

Before we say anything else

When we started this club, we hoped a handful of teachers might enjoy a puzzle on a Saturday morning. We didn’t expect a community.

But that is exactly what you built. Every time you solved something before your first cup of coffee finished, forwarded a puzzle to a colleague down the hall, or told us it ended up sketched on your classroom board — you turned a small idea into something we’re genuinely proud of.

Teaching is the hardest kind of problem-solving there is, done every single day, for other people’s children, often with far too little thanks. So today, just for a moment, we wanted to turn that around.

Thank you for showing up. Thank you for staying curious. Thank you for being the kind of teacher who still makes time for a good puzzle.

With gratitude, The LogIQids Team

Looking Ahead

What’s for the coming year

Surprise Reveal

A Pocket Companion for Educators

Computational Thinking is becoming an essential skill for every student. As AI and CT find their place in classrooms, teachers need practical, classroom-ready resources to bring these concepts to life. Drawing on over a decade of experience in Computational Thinking and Logical Reasoning, we created this community to bridge the gap between curriculum and classroom.

Over the past year, you turned those resources into meaningful classroom experiences. Every worksheet explored, every strategy implemented, and every discussion sparked has helped make learning richer for countless students. Thank you for your trust, your curiosity, and your commitment to continuous learning. It has been a privilege to be a part of your journey.

Your support and encouragement inspired us to take the next step.

Introducing the LogIQids For Teachers app—a dedicated platform to help educators confidently learn, implement, and teach Computational Thinking at their own pace.

The app includes:
📄 Exclusive LogIQids worksheets
🎓 Four CPD-compliant professional development courses
🧩 Daily CT quizzes and thinking challenges
🤝 Help from the LogIQids Academic Team
📘 Grade-wise CT modules aligned with CBSE handbooks

Thank you for making this first year truly meaningful. Here's to empowering more teachers, inspiring more young thinkers, and growing together in the years ahead.

🔑 Campus Access: Simply reach out to your LogIQids School Relationship Manager to unlock complimentary access for your educators and receive your onboarding steps!
